# Shipping and delivery service levels

## Estimated delivery date
Every order shows an estimated delivery date at checkout. The estimate depends on the seller's state, the customer's state and the product weight. Orders to BA, PE and GO usually take 5 days longer because of carrier routes.

## Late delivery definition
An order is late when the customer receives it after the estimated delivery date. Late delivery is the strongest driver of 1-star reviews.

## Compensation for late orders
When an order arrives more than 7 days after the estimated date, the customer receives a voucher worth 10% of the order value. Orders more than 20 days late may be cancelled for a full refund.

## Carrier escalation
Sellers must hand orders to the carrier before the shipping limit date. Repeated misses are reported to the seller quality program.
