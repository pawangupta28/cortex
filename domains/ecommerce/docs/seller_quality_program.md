# Seller quality program

## Scoring
Each seller is scored monthly on average review score, late delivery rate and cancellation rate.

## Thresholds
A seller with an average review score below 3.5 over the last 90 days is placed on probation. A late delivery rate above 15% triggers a written warning. Two warnings in six months suspend the seller's new listings.

## Packaging requirements for fragile categories
Sellers in furniture_decor and office_furniture must use double-wall boxes and corner protection. Damage complaints in these categories are reviewed weekly.
