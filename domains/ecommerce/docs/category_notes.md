# Category operating notes

## Furniture and office furniture
These categories have the highest freight cost and the most damage claims. Large items travel through more carrier hubs, which raises both delay and breakage risk.

## Auto parts
Most auto returns are fitment problems. Listings must state the compatible car models and years, and the support team checks compatibility before shipping.

## Health and beauty
Products must have at least 6 months of shelf life left when shipped. Items closer to expiry must be removed from sale.

## Electronics and telephony
Defect claims are handled under the 90-day legal warranty. Sellers must test chargers and cables before dispatch.
