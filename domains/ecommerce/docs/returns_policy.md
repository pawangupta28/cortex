# Returns and refunds policy

## Right of withdrawal
Customers may return any product within 7 days of delivery without giving a reason. The return shipping cost is paid by the store when the return is made within this window.

## Damaged items
If a product arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 7 days of delivery and attach photos of the item and the packaging. The store sends a replacement or a full refund, at the customer's choice. Damaged furniture is collected by the carrier at no cost to the customer.

## Defective items
Products that stop working within 90 days are covered by the legal warranty. The customer opens a ticket, and the seller has 30 days to repair or replace the item before a refund is due.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are paid within 10 business days to the original payment method. Vouchers are issued within 2 business days.
